## Phase Goal

**As a** TopFit operator, **I want to** have a fresh, normalised, embedded index of real India/remote tech jobs that one worker refreshes on a schedule without ever wiping it, **so that** search and ranking in later phases work on real, current jobs even when the LLM is unavailable.

<objective>
Slice: "closed jobs leave the index, but an outage never empties it". Per-source expiry (absence for full feeds, TTL for Adzuna) with the 50% volume guard (ING-08), the simple same-key dedupe rule (CONTEXT: full ING-10 is Phase 6), and the source purge command needed by Adzuna's termination clause.

Output: `ingest/expiry.py`, `ingest/dedupe.py`, pipeline hooks, `ranker purge-source` and `ranker expire-ttl` commands, tests.
</objective>
