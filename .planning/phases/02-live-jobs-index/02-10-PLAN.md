## Phase Goal

**As a** TopFit operator, **I want to** have a fresh, normalised, embedded index of real India/remote tech jobs that one worker refreshes on a schedule without ever wiping it, **so that** search and ranking in later phases work on real, current jobs even when the LLM is unavailable.

<objective>
Slice: "Adzuna India volume flows into the same index". Adds the Adzuna adapter as a plugin (ING-04) with the category + date-sorted crawl plan and quota budget measured in ING-00 (ING-02), backfill mode with a resumable cursor, and the key-leak protections that the Adzuna query-string credentials require (ING-07 failure path, Pitfall 1).

Output: `ingest/sources/adzuna.py`, `sources.yaml` adzuna section, Settings keys, redaction changes, tests.
</objective>
